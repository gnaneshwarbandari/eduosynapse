Our Legacy & Purpose

About EDUOSYNAPSE

Committed to bridging the academic-industry divide by introducing school students to the core practical pillars of the Fourth Industrial Revolution.

Our Vision

To become one of India's leading technology education providers by empowering school students with future-ready skills through experiential learning in IoT, Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, Programming, and emerging technologies.

Our Mission

  • Deliver high-quality practical technology education tailored for early skill adoption.
  • Encourage real-world innovation through project-based learning and working hardware.
  • Make students industry-ready through immersive hands-on STEM engineering frameworks.

Our Learning Methodology

Designed to maximize engagement and hardware mastery

20% Core Theory

Basic Electronics, Logic Building, Circuit Diagrams, & Fundamentals.

80% Practical Execution

Write code, construct prototypes, debug errors, & launch physical hardware.
