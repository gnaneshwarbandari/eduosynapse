Hands-On Learning Track

IoT Bootcamp Curriculum (VI–X)

A progressive, practical syllabus designed to evolve students from fundamental electronics to advanced IoT system automation.

Class VI: Fundamentals

  • Blinking LED & Button Control: Understand digital outputs, circuits, and breadboard logic.
  • Buzzer Alert System: Learn electrical-to-sound energy conversion and frequency control.
  • Digital Dice & Electronic Quiz: Practice random logic generation and event-based execution.
  • Traffic Signal Simulator: Program timed multi-LED state sequencing.

Class VII: Sensing World

  • Ultrasonic Distance Measurement: Master sound wave reflection principles and range mapping.
  • Obstacle Detection Alarm: Trigger automated visual & acoustic alerts based on proximity thresholds.
  • Servo Motor Angle Control: Program precise rotational motion and pulse-width modulation.
  • Automatic Barrier Gate: Integrate distance sensors directly with mechanical servo arms.

Class VIII: Environmental Tech

  • Gas Leakage Detector: Monitor hazardous gas concentrations for automated safety response.
  • Soil Moisture Tester: Measure volumetric water content for smart agriculture applications.
  • Smart Plant Watering Alert: Trigger automated notifications when soil dryness thresholds pass.
  • Multi-Sensor Safety System: Combine diverse environmental variables into conditional multi-point safety logic.

Class IX: Automation

  • Automatic Dustbin (Servo): Touchless waste lid actuation using ultrasonic hands-free sensing.
  • Smart Water Tank Indicator: Monitor fluid levels accurately to eliminate overflow water wastage.
  • Smart Irrigation Prototype: Soil condition monitoring linked with active relay-driven water pumps.
  • Intelligent Parking System: Space occupancy tracking with gate barriers and slot feedback.
