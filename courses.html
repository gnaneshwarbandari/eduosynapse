Sample Curriculum

IoT Curriculum (VI–X)

A progressive, practical syllabus from fundamental electronics to advanced IoT automation. Tap a class to explore.

Class VI: Fundamentals

Blinking LED & Button Control

Digital outputs, circuits and breadboard logic.

Buzzer Alert System

Electrical-to-sound energy conversion and frequency control.

Digital Dice & Electronic Quiz

Random logic generation and event-based execution.

Traffic Signal Simulator

Timed multi-LED state sequencing.

Class VII: Sensing World

Ultrasonic Distance Measurement

Sound wave reflection principles and range mapping.

Obstacle Detection Alarm

Visual and acoustic alerts based on proximity thresholds.

Servo Motor Angle Control

Precise rotational motion and pulse-width modulation.

Automatic Barrier Gate

Distance sensors integrated with mechanical servo arms.

Class VIII: Environmental Tech

Gas Leakage Detector

Monitor hazardous gas concentrations for automated safety response.

Soil Moisture Tester

Measure volumetric water content for smart agriculture.

Smart Plant Watering Alert

Notifications when soil dryness thresholds pass.

Multi-Sensor Safety System

Combine environmental variables into multi-point safety logic.

Class IX: Automation

Automatic Dustbin (Servo)

Touchless waste lid actuation using ultrasonic sensing.

Smart Water Tank Indicator

Monitor fluid levels to eliminate overflow wastage.

Smart Irrigation Prototype

Soil monitoring linked with relay-driven water pumps.

Intelligent Parking System

Space occupancy tracking with gate barriers and slot feedback.

Class X: System IntegrationCapstone

Smart Home Automation

Residential smart ecosystems with wireless relay switching, light sensing and automated safety response.

Industrial Gas Safety

Industrial-grade monitors with automated exhaust triggering and remote hazard alarms.

Intelligent Entry Control

Multi-factor access gates using security sensors, actuators and status displays.

Mini Smart City Model

Street lighting, safety, water management and environmental monitoring in one master prototype.
