100% Quality Inspected & Testing Policy

Every electronic component supplied by EDUOSYNAPSE is individually bench-tested prior to workshop distribution. However, due to the sensitive nature of microcontrollers and potential student handling errors (e.g., static discharge, wiring short-circuits), hardware components do not carry post-use warranties or guarantees. Minor consumable replacements will be managed seamlessly on-site during active training sessions.
